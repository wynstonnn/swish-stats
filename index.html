Update the stats

Your Excel tracker is the source. Enter the new game in Excel as usual, save, then drop the file here.

  1. Fill in Games (opponent, result, both scores) and Player_Data (one row per player) for the new game.
  2. Optional: add shots to Shot_Data so the court heatmaps fill in, and new names to Player_Roster.
  3. Save the .xlsx and choose it below. Check the summary, then publish.
