How the numbers work

Player averages are sums divided by the number of Player_Data records for that player. Shooting percentages use total makes divided by total attempts, rather than averaging percentages. Missing values are shown as —; averages use only records where that stat was recorded. No-attempt percentages are —.

Progress compares only records with valid dates from 2000 onward. Placeholder dates stay in the all-record averages and game log, but are excluded from time-based claims. Same-day records are shown in workbook order.

Team results come from Games. A missing score is excluded from the win/loss record. Box-score totals are checked against the official SWISH score. No personal ages or birth years are displayed.
